## The intended outcome

Provous is intended to let an AI agent pay without putting the payer, recipient, amount, or reason on public display. The project is built around Zcash shielded technology.

That is a design goal, not a completed privacy analysis of the Provous implementation. The exact transaction path and guarantees must be confirmed against the working tool and its threat model.

## What this page can say today

| Question | Current answer |
| --- | --- |
| What is Provous trying to protect? | The payment relationship and its details: who paid, who was paid, how much, and why. |
| What technology is it built around? | Zcash shielded technology. |
| Has the complete Provous flow been independently verified here? | No. This frontend has not received an implementation contract or audit results. |
| Are network, timing, or endpoint metadata hidden? | Not established. Do not assume they are protected by the payment flow. |

## What still needs to be documented

- Which transaction details are shielded, and under what conditions.
- What information a recipient, network observer, RPC provider, or operator can see.
- How wallet keys are stored, used, backed up, and recovered.
- What happens when funds move between transparent and shielded pools.
- The effects of timing, network metadata, and application logs.
- Whether spending policies are enforced in a way the agent cannot bypass.

## A practical rule

Do not use real funds or rely on a privacy guarantee based only on this page. Wait for the tool-specific threat model and test the exact release you plan to use.
